London-based audio engineer, producer and educator.

I’m a London-based audio-engineer, producer, and educator working across recording, production, and artist development. I work closely with artists to help them realise the sound they’re aiming for, from early demos through to the finished record.

Alongside my studio work, I’ve run and taught in the professional sound studio at London South Bank University, leading sessions and supporting artists through the record making process.

I offer production, mixing, mastering and session support. If you’d like to explore working together, you can book a free consultation to discuss your project and next steps.
