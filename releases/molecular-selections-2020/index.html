Audio-visual label Nudibranch Records present their first compilation album, Nudibranch - Molecular Selections.

Molecular Selections is the first edition in a series of compilations exploring Nudibranch’s sci-fi world. This installment tells the story of The Cephalogods, an ancient microscopic species who lay down the foundations for life and civilisations to grow on alien planets. They spend their time in deep-space utopian bliss, weaving different permutations of life-code to disseminate across the universe, all just to observe the results.

This eclectic compilation is an homage to our tiny octopus overlords. Whilst terraforming galaxies is outside of our mortal capabilities, as artists and musicians we do have the ability to create life in the form of Balearic slow jams, cosmic Disco and futuristic acid-electro. Best enjoyed from responsibly distanced bean bags or private listening pods, this is armchair-dance music to soothe earthlings during these turbulent times.

Featuring music from label-founders Polyop, Ratomagoson, Macaulay Sulkin, Gorzian, and newcomers Cyphon, cos(mos), Prince Taurus, Tulloch and Tom George, and 3D visuals from Polyop’s Alex Kempton. The release draws attention to the vibrant and co-operative community of artists and musicians growing together within the label.
