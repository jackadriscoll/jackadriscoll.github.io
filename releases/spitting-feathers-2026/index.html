Spitting Feathers

Elijah Minnelli

2026 12" EP - Breadminster County Council
  • Mastering

Elijah Minnelli returns with another dose of folk-inspired dub on his Breadminster County Council imprint. Spitting Feathers follows the Ball & Socket EP and the albums Clams As A Main Meal and Perpetual Musket.

The four-track EP builds on the weary, handwrought tactility of those releases, mining old-world melody and dub methodology. Across the record, soundsystem improvisation, Casio-led digital dub, early Bristol dubstep and the Appalachian ballad “Vandy” meet Minnelli’s mournful theme of exile.

“Full of tiny, dynamically filtered twists, it’s seriously spaced out but still seriously groovy.”

Ban Ban Ton Ton

“Neither side gives way… and that friction produces the most fully realised track on the EP.”

Hwael Mag
